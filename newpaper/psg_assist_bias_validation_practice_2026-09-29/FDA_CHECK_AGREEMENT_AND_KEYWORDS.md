## Keyword search for statements on how reference scorers worked

- **When and how.** Run on 11 October 2026 with PyMuPDF on the cached summary PDFs of the 25 clearances since 2012 (`outputs/psg_assist_bias_validation_practice/pdf/`, not tracked). It searched the text of every page, case-insensitively, for two pattern families:
  - **Workflow:** `pre-?scor\w*`, `prescor\w*`, `semi-?automat\w*`, `from scratch`, `de novo`, `unassisted`, `without (the )?(aid|assistance|use) of`, `(edit|correct)\w* (the )?(automatic|auto|device)\w*`, `computer[- ]assist\w*`, `manual(ly)? (edit|correct|review)\w*`.
  - **Blinding:** `blind\w*`, `unaware`, `masked`, `no access`, `without access`, `(did )?not have access`.
- **Result.** No summary contains a statement on whether the reference scorers worked from automatic pre-scoring by any software. The hits fall into five groups:
  - **K253390 (SOMNUM, 2026).** "independently generated by three RPSGT-certified PSG technologists ... without access to SOMNUM automatic scoring output" (p. 15). This concerns the device's own output only, coded `device output only`.
  - **K210034 (EnsoSleep, 2021).** "scoring-blind" controls and a "double-blind, prospective 2/3 Majority Scoring panel consensus reference of independent ... scorers" (pp. 13, 24). The object of the blinding is not stated, coded `stated (object unspecified)`.
  - **Intended workflow of the device or its predicates.** Manual review or verification of the automatic scoring (for example K192469, K223539, K241960, K252330, K250851, K253668), and K153412's flagging of epochs for manual review. These describe how the device is used, not how the reference was made. Four of these clearances (K192469, K221179, K241960, K252330) used routine clinical scoring as the reference. Laboratories that use such software may score with assistance, but the summaries do not say so.
  - **Device descriptions.** The "computer-assisted scoring modules" of K153353, K162140, K163617 and K173366.
  - **No relevant hit** in the remaining summaries.
- **Limit.** A keyword search can miss a statement worded in other terms. It corroborates the human check; it is not the blind second coding.
