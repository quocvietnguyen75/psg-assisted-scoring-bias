# Abstract cut for the submission form's 250-word limit (4 November 2026)

At the abstract step of the Wiley submission, the form reported "Your current selection uses 317 of 250 words allowed".
The JSR author guidelines saved on 8 October give no abstract word limit; the form enforces 250. The abstract was cut
by deleting three whole sentences and adding one word, so that no kept sentence changed and no claim was added
(the corresponding author approved in the submission session; the co-authors had approved the longer version).

Rule used: keep what the Conclusions and the cover letter's "What this article adds" carry (closer than the same experts'
unassisted scoring; favored over stagers the experts never saw; the recommendations), the design and context, and the
FDA sentence (the evidence for the reporting recommendation); delete the rest. The kept sentences that earlier reviews
shaped are unchanged: the estimand (amendment 005 Part E, R1), "Under dated, unregistered analysis plans" and "for
these experts and recordings" (S13, S14), the FDA sentence's "since 2012", "product code OLZ (...)" and "by any
software" (L5, R11), "In this dataset" (Part E), and the exposure condition of the recommendation (R5).

Deleted, verbatim as printed:

1. "Where the consensus changed between sessions, it took the pre-scoring’s label in 79% to 99% of the changed units (epochs for stages, 0.1-s bins for events)."
2. "A stager that had been level with the pre-scoring against the manual reference, ahead in 3 of 5 recordings, trailed it in all five."
3. "In an emulation with the earlier session’s unassisted labels, an audit of 10% of 30-minute blocks estimated the staging comparison without this bias (95% half-width 0.045; 0.059 with the audit’s own degrees of freedom)."

Added: "30-minute" before "blocks" in the closing recommendation, whose antecedent was in sentence 3 (the Discussion's
own wording, "a random sample of 30-minute blocks").
