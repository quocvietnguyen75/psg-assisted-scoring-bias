# STARD 2015 reporting checklist

Manuscript: "Polysomnography reference standards built by editing automatic pre-scoring favored the assisting algorithm". Page numbers refer to the main document as built on 3 November 2026 (page 1 is the
title page).

**How STARD applies.** This is a methodological study of reference standards, not a classical diagnostic accuracy
study. The index tests are automatic scoring algorithms: the pre-fill that the experts edited, and two independent
stagers that no expert saw (YASA and GSSC). The target is the label of each epoch or scored event. Accuracy is agreement
(Cohen's kappa for stages, event-level F1 for events) with two expert consensus references: one made without the
pre-fill (manual) and one made by editing it (assisted). Items about patients' clinical characteristics are reported
as far as the open dataset documents them.
